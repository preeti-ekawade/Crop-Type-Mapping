Mapping crops in Shahbad, Haryana with radar

Kharif 2025 crop map made from Sentinel-1 radar (VV and VH backscatter) in 12-day windows and a Random Forest classifier. Radar sees through monsoon clouds, so it can follow crops through the whole season. Accuracy is checked on fields held out from training.

Limitations and responsible use

Reference points were hand-labelled using Sentinel-2 time series, not all verified in the field, so accuracy is indicative. Accuracy is measured on held-out spatial blocks, not a random probability sample. Areas are pixel counts and are not corrected for classification error. "Other crop" mixes several crops and is the least certain class. Do not use this map for individual-field decisions, insurance or legal claims.
