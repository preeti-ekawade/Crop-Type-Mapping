Reading the landscape from space.

First, find Shahbad.

Shahbad Markanda lies in Kurukshetra district, Haryana, a region with an important agricultural landscape. Mapping crop types can help describe where different crop patterns occur during the Kharif season.

Begin with the study boundary, then explore how satellite observations can reveal variation across agricultural fields.

Next: Signals
2

Every crop leaves a different trace.

Radar and optical satellites observe different properties of the land. Sentinel-1 radar can collect observations through clouds, while optical imagery can help track vegetation condition when cloud-free data are available.

PaddyEarly-season surface water or flooding may be followed by strong vegetation growth.
SugarcaneOften shows a longer-lasting vegetation signal; patterns depend on crop stage and field conditions.
Other cropSeasonal green-up that does not match the target crop patterns.
Fallow / non-agriculturalMay lack a consistent crop-growth signal during the observation period.

How each class looks to radar

Class-mean VH backscatter (dB) across Kharif 2025, in 12-day steps. Tap a class to hide or show it.

Paddy has the clearest signature. Its VH backscatter falls to about −29 dB in late June, consistent with flooded fields, then climbs as the crop grows. Fallow and non-agricultural land stays highest and flattest, around −15 dB.

These are typical interpretation cues, not universal rules. Crop signals vary with planting dates, irrigation, soil moisture and management.

Where the crops are

Share of mapped area by class. Tap a class to highlight it.

Paddy covers almost half the mapped area. Sugarcane is next at 21.4%; fallow / non-agricultural land and other crops are each near 15–16%.

How accurate is the map?

Checked on held-out points. Producer's: how much of each real class was found. User's: how often a mapped class is right. F1 combines both.

Paddy is mapped most reliably; other crop is the weakest class. Treat other-crop results with extra care and check them against field data.

For trustworthy results, calculate class-wise area from the classified raster and report accuracy only when independent validation data are available.
Next: Impact
5

From a map to better questions.

A crop-type map can support seasonal crop monitoring, agricultural surveys, irrigation planning and precision-agriculture studies. It can also help identify places where additional field checks or better reference data are needed.

The map is not a substitute for field verification. Its usefulness depends on input data quality, the timing of observations, training samples and independent accuracy assessment.
